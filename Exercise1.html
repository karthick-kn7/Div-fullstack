The Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras or IIT-M) is a public technical university located in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. It is one of the eight public Institutes of Eminence of India. As an Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), IIT Madras is also recognized as an Institute of National Importance.

Knowledge Institute of Technology (KIOT) is the brain child of eminent professors from the leading Engineering Colleges, budding entrepreneurs and friends with a vision to build our Nation through quality education. The Institution was established in the year 2009 and affiliated to Anna University, Chennai, approved by AICTE, New Delhi. The Institution offers B.E. Programs in EEE, CSE, ECE, MECH & CIVIL and M.E. Programs in Embedded System Technologies, Computer Science and Engineering,Industrial Safety Engineering and VLSI Design. Knowledge Business School (KBS) is the sister Institution situated in the same KIOT campus, offers MBA Programs. Holistic teaching, well equipped class rooms, User friendly laboratories with air conditioners, High profile placement records, R&D cell and well established Central library are the highlights of the institution.
